  \vspace*{2.0cm}
  {\Large \textsc{LHCb Open Data Analysis}\par}
  \vspace{1.2cm}
  {\huge\bfseries A CERN Open Data Analysis of Three-Body B-Meson Decays\par}
  \vspace{0.5cm}
  {\Large Invariant-Mass Fits, Raw Charge Asymmetry, and Dalitz-Plot Diagnostics\par}
  \vspace{1.8cm}
  {\large Yanis Idir\par}
  \vfill
  \begin{abstract}
  This report presents a compact but realistic high-energy-physics analysis workflow based on LHCb CERN Open Data. Three-body $B$-meson candidates are reconstructed from charged-hadron tracks under a kaon mass hypothesis, selected with transparent quality and particle-identification requirements, and their invariant-mass spectrum is fitted by extended binned maximum likelihood. Fitting the \Bplus{} and \Bminus{} spectra simultaneously with shared shapes, on both magnet polarities, and averaging the polarities gives a raw charge asymmetry of $-0.0321 \pm 0.0213\,\mathrm{(stat.)} \pm 0.0163\,\mathrm{(syst.)}$, where the systematic uncertainty is the joint envelope of mass-fit model and fitted-range variations under pre-registered acceptance criteria, and where the statistical procedure is validated on $2000$ pseudo-experiments. A low-mass excess in one charge spectrum of one polarity is shown, with the independent polarity, to be a fluctuation, and the simultaneous fit removes the sensitivity to it that separate fits had. The workflow is completed by a sideband-labelled classifier study, Dalitz-plot distributions, and a bin-by-bin local asymmetry diagnostic across the three-body phase space. The asymmetry is raw: no detector, production, trigger or efficiency corrections are applied, and it is presented as a diagnostic of the analysis chain rather than as a measurement of CP violation. The report emphasizes reproducibility, modular scientific code, and explicit statements of what each result does and does not establish.
  \end{abstract}
